Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how LMR Capitals Pty. Ltd. ("LMR Capitals", "we", "us") handles personal information in the LMR Capitals app — the trading journal available at lmrcapitals.com/app and in the LMR Capitals app for iPhone, iPad and Mac ("the App").

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider)To create and secure your account and let you sign in
Journal content you enter: trades, trading accounts, transactions and payouts, daily/weekly/monthly analysis, notes, journal entries, psychology and observation entries, and images you attachTo provide the journal and sync it across your devices
Profile details you choose to add (name, photo, preferences)To personalise the App
Subscription status (plan, trial and renewal dates)To give paying subscribers access

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered with and held by Stripe — they never reach our servers. We receive only your subscription status and a customer reference.

Optional features that send data to other services

  • AI Coach and AI reviews — if you add your own Anthropic (Claude) API key, the journal data needed to answer your request is sent directly to Anthropic under your key and their terms. Your key is saved to your profile so it works on your other devices.
  • Discord sharing — if you set up a Discord webhook, the posts you choose to share are sent to that Discord channel.

Nothing is sent to these services unless you turn the feature on.

Where your data is stored

Account and journal data is stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider) and a copy is cached on your device so the App works quickly and offline. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure each account can only read and change its own data. Our providers may process data outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When your account is deleted, your journal data and account are removed from our database; backups held by our providers are overwritten on their normal cycle. Payment records may be kept by Stripe as required by law.

Your choices and rights

  • View, edit or delete any journal entry at any time in the App.
  • Export your trades from the App.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and data by emailing admin@lmrcapitals.com.

Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the App.
