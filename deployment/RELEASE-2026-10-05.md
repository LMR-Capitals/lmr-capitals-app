## Rollback

If the new frontend fails sign-in or loading, restore Netlify deploy `6ac36fe93ee38b0008cac1bb` through the existing site's deploy dashboard. Verify `/`, `/app` and `/admin` after restoring. Leave the additive database schema in place: it preserves existing rows/policies and removing new tables could destroy learning/member data created after launch. Do not drop tables as a frontend rollback.

Ignored local recovery artifacts are in `.local-preview/deployment/`: preflight policies/function definitions and previous Stripe function source responses. They were not included in `dist` or uploaded. These are targeted schema/function snapshots, not a full database backup.
