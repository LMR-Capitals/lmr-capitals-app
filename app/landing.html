How We Do It

The LMR Methodology

A repeatable framework that links every day's decisions to the bigger picture — built so that nothing is left to gut feeling.

01 — DAILY

The Daily Chain

Each day begins with a defined bias, key HTF points of interest, and a session plan — then closes with a full review of execution, P&L, and lessons before the next day begins.

02 — WEEKLY

The Weekly Chain

Every week is mapped against the MMBM and MMSM weekly models, identifying the dominant profile and how price is expected to deliver across the week.

03 — MONTHLY

The Monthly Chain

Monthly analysis tracks the broader quarterly shift (STS/LTS bias) and market profile — trending, retracing, consolidating, or manipulative — to keep daily decisions aligned with the bigger picture.

04 — SESSIONS

Session Profiling

London and New York sessions are each classified — Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution, or Rebalance/Reversal, Manipulation/Distribution, Retracement/Continuation — to anticipate how price should move before it moves.

05 — LANGUAGE & MIND

LMR Terminology & Psychology

A shared vocabulary and psychological framework — covering market structure language and emotional management — keeps decision-making consistent and removes ambiguity from every analysis.

06 — DISCIPLINE

Daily Discipline & Journaling

Every trade is tagged with its model, session, emotion, and outcome. Patterns — good and bad — surface automatically, turning raw history into actionable feedback.
